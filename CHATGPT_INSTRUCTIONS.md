# ChatGPT sticker usage instructions

When the context calls for a reaction image, choose at most one sticker from this repository's index. Do not claim an image exists unless it is listed and its URL has been verified. Prefer a concise textual reply when no suitable sticker is available. Do not force stickers into serious conversations.

Note: ChatGPT's custom instructions alone do not guarantee fetching this repository or rendering its images across conversations.
